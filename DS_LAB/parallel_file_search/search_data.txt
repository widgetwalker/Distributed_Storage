A gunshot cracked through the air, splintering a tree trunk just ahead. Maya flinched, ducking instinctively as Rohan swore under his breath. “They’re too close,” he muttered, scanning the dense foliage. “We need cover -now.”

He veered left, leading them toward a gnarled cluster of trees where the undergrowth thickened into a natural shield. They crashed through, thorns tearing at their clothes, and dropped into a shallow hollow beneath a massive banyan, its sprawling roots draping over them like a ragged curtain. Maya pressed herself against the damp earth, Arjun beside her, their breaths mingling in the tight space. Rohan crouched at the edge, peering through the gaps, his body taut with tension.
